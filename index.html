01 — The Market

The market buys independently.
The supply chain doesn't have to.

Clinics and distributors typically negotiate alone. Purchasing stays fragmented, and the strongest terms go to whoever already buys at scale.

Manufacturers face the same problem in reverse — many small, disconnected orders instead of concentrated, qualified demand.

08 — Operating Principles

Built on procurement discipline, not sales volume.

Direct sourcing

Product is sourced directly from Korean manufacturers, keeping the supply chain short and traceable.

Cold-chain & documentation

Shipments are verified and customs-documented before they leave Korea, not after they arrive.

Consolidated logistics

Orders are combined into single freight runs, reducing the per-unit cost of moving product internationally.

Commercial transparency

Pricing reflects consolidated volume, not a chain of undisclosed intermediary margins.
