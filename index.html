01 — The Market

The market buys independently.
The supply chain doesn't have to.

Clinics and distributors typically negotiate alone. Purchasing stays fragmented, and the strongest terms go to whoever already buys at scale.

Manufacturers face the same problem in reverse — many small, disconnected orders instead of concentrated, qualified demand.

02 — The Mechanism

How aggregation works.

  1. 01

    Aggregate

    Fragmented purchasing demand from clinics and distributors is brought together inside one network.

  2. 02

    Access

    Aggregated demand is used to build stronger commercial relationships and access across the supply chain.

  3. 03

    Scale

    As participation grows, the network becomes more valuable — to buyers and supply-side partners alike.

03 — The Network

One network.
Multiple points of leverage.

Manufacturing capacity and distributor inventory sit on one side. Clinics and buyers sit on the other — and CellTonis is the connective layer that turns many small orders into fewer, larger, better-negotiated ones.

04 — Participants

Built for three sides of the same market.

Clinics

More purchasing power.
Less procurement friction.

Clinics join the network without needing the purchasing scale of a major group — participating in aggregated demand rather than negotiating alone.

Distributors

Concentrated demand.
Qualified buyers.

Distributors gain access to structured, downstream demand from clinics already inside the network — rather than sourcing individual buyers one at a time.

Manufacturers

Connect capacity to demand.

As the network expands, it creates a long-term path for manufacturers to connect production capacity with a growing base of qualified buyers.

07 — Vision

Today, we consolidate orders.
Tomorrow, we consolidate markets.

The immediate objective is a strong base of clinics and distributors inside one network. As participation grows, aggregated demand becomes more meaningful — and the network can extend across additional regions and markets.

08 — Operating Principles

Built on procurement discipline, not sales volume.

Direct sourcing

Product is sourced directly from Korean manufacturers, keeping the supply chain short and traceable.

Cold-chain & documentation

Shipments are verified and customs-documented before they leave Korea, not after they arrive.

Consolidated logistics

Orders are combined into single freight runs, reducing the per-unit cost of moving product internationally.

Commercial transparency

Pricing reflects consolidated volume, not a chain of undisclosed intermediary margins.
