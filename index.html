The Problem

Every layer between the factory and your clinic is a markup you're paying for.

Local agents. Regional resellers. A second distributor clearing customs for a third. By the time product reaches a clinic in Riyadh, Almaty, or London, its price has passed through hands that never touched a factory floor.

CellTonis removes the layers. One supply agreement, one consolidated shipment, one price — set at the factory gate and passed through, not marked up.

How It Works

Consolidation is the mechanic. Everything else follows from it.

  1. 01

    Source

    Direct supply agreements with GMP-certified Korean manufacturers. No broker sits between us and the factory.

  2. 02

    Consolidate

    Orders from clinics and distributors across five markets are combined into single freight runs, unlocking factory-tier pricing without factory-tier minimums.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Cold-chain integrity and customs documentation are confirmed before a shipment ever leaves Korea.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Cleared, verified, and delivered to your clinic or distribution point — on a schedule you can plan around.
